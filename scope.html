Scope, boundaries, and terms

Know exactly what is reviewed, what is delivered, and what this service does not claim.

Included

  • One agreed JavaScript/TypeScript repository state, up to 150,000 lines of first-party source.
  • Repository, configuration, dependency, install, typecheck, lint, test, build, and deployment evidence where safely available.
  • Targeted review of secrets, authentication, authorization, API/data handling, payments, errors, and deployment assumptions.
  • Browser, responsive, and basic automated accessibility observations when a safe runnable URL and required tooling are available.
  • Founder-readable HTML and Markdown report with launch classification, blockers, priorities, evidence, and NOT_TESTED areas.
  • One 30-minute clarification call or one written clarification round.

Not included

  • Penetration testing, exploitation, social engineering, denial-of-service, or destructive testing.
  • Compliance, legal, privacy, accessibility, payment, or security certification.
  • A guarantee that an application is secure, vulnerability-free, ready for production, or free of undiscovered bugs.
  • Source-code fixes, architecture redesign, deployment, production changes, monitoring, or incident response.
  • Additional repositories, native mobile binaries, or unsupported languages unless separately agreed.

Service terms

Evidence boundary

The assessment applies only to the repository state, configuration evidence, URL, roles, and review period identified in the final report. Changes made after review can invalidate conclusions.

Safe testing

Testing is non-destructive and limited to the authorized repository and agreed non-production environment. Tripex does not exploit vulnerabilities, alter production data, process real payments, or access unrelated systems.

Customer responsibility

The customer confirms authorization, provides accurate intake information and safe test access, protects credentials, evaluates recommendations, and remains responsible for launch and regulatory decisions.

Delivery guarantee

If Tripex accepts an application as in scope and cannot deliver the agreed report within 48 hours after complete intake and access, the customer may choose a full refund.
